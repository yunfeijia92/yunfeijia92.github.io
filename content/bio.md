I am a PhD student in the Department of Energy, Environmental & Chemical Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by [**Prof. Jenna C. Ditto**](https://engineering.washu.edu/faculty/Jenna-Ditto.html). I received my MS from Tongji University in 2020 under the supervision of [**Prof. Weiguo Zhou**](https://mefaculty.tongji.edu.cn/info/1373/3771.htm), together with a minor from the UNEP-Tongji Institute of Environment for Sustainable Development. Prior to that, I obtained my BS from Shanghai University of Electric Power in 2015. Prior to pursuing my PhD, I worked as a mechanical and electrical engineer at a coal-fired power plant in Shandong, China, from 2015 to 2017. I later worked in the Department of Urban Gas Transmission and Distribution at Shanghai Gas in Shanghai, China, from 2020 to 2024, where I started as a management trainee, was then promoted to Assistant Manager, and later to Associate Manager.

My research focuses on occupant-environment interactions, with particular emphasis on indoor air chemistry, heterogeneous chemistry, and human exposure. I use mass spectrometry to characterize the chemical evolution and spatial transport of volatile/semivolatile organic compounds in indoor environments. My current research projects include the transformation and transport of thirdhand tobacco smoke, the re-emission and cross-space transport of gas-phase chemicals from fabrics, and the chemical transformation of house dust mite allergens.
