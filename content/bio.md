I am a PhD student in the Department of Energy, Environmental & Chemical Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by [**Prof. Jenna C. Ditto**](https://engineering.washu.edu/faculty/Jenna-Ditto.html). I received my MS from Tongji University in 2020 under the supervision of [**Prof. Weiguo Zhou**](https://autoen.tongji.edu.cn/Faculty/FacultyStaff/aszm.htm), together with a minor from the UNEP-Tongji Institute of Environment for Sustainable Development. Prior to that, I obtained my BEng from Shanghai University of Electric Power in 2015. Prior to pursuing my PhD, I worked as a mechanical and electrical engineer at a coal-fired power plant in Shandong, China, from 2015 to 2017. I later worked in the Department of Urban Gas Transmission and Distribution at Shanghai Gas in Shanghai, China, from 2020 to 2024, where I served as an Assistant Manager and later as an Associate Manager.

My research focuses on occupant–environment interactions, with particular emphasis on indoor air chemistry, heterogeneous chemistry, and human exposure. My current research projects focus on thirdhand tobacco smoke transformation and transport, re-emission of gaseous compounds from fabrics, and the chemical transformation of house dust mite allergens.
